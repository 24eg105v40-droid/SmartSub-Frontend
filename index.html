Health Score Calculation Formula

SmartSub evaluates every subscription on a deterministic 0–100 scale to ensure objective financial transparency:

1. Usage Frequency (40% Weight)

(Days Used per Month ÷ 30) × 100. Directly rewards active daily utility.

2. Value & Satisfaction Rating (30% Weight)

Rating (1–5 Stars) × 20. Captures subjective personal necessity and enjoyment.

3. Cost Impact & Budget Efficiency (30% Weight)

Evaluates cost burden: ≤₹300/mo (95pts), ≤₹800/mo (75pts), ≤₹1,500/mo (55pts), >₹1,500/mo (35pts).

Score Ranges:

  • 75 – 100 (Excellent): Optimal usage and superior return on investment.
  • 55 – 74 (Good): Well-balanced utility and reasonable cost.
  • 35 – 54 (Needs Review): High cost relative to recorded active days.
  • 0 – 34 (High Attention): Seldom used with significant monthly drag.
